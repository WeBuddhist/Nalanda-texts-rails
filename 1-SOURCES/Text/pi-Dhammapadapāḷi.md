# Dhammapadapāḷi ^0

Namo tassa bhagavato arahato sammāsambuddhassa ^1

<h1>Khuddakanikāye</h1> ^2

<h1>Dhammapadapāḷi</h1> ^3

<h3>1. Yamakavaggo</h3> ^4

Manopubbaṅgamā dhammā, manoseṭṭhā manomayā; ⤵ Manasā ce paduṭṭhena, bhāsati vā karoti vā; ⤵ Tato naṃ dukkhamanveti, cakkaṃva vahato padaṃ. ^5

Manopubbaṅgamā dhammā, manoseṭṭhā manomayā; ⤵ Manasā ce pasannena, bhāsati vā karoti vā; ⤵ Tato naṃ sukhamanveti, chāyāva anapāyinī [anupāyinī (ka.)]. ^6

Akkocchi maṃ avadhi maṃ, ajini [ajinī (?)] maṃ ahāsi me; ⤵ Ye ca taṃ upanayhanti, veraṃ tesaṃ na sammati. ^7

Akkocchi maṃ avadhi maṃ, ajini maṃ ahāsi me; ⤵ Ye ca taṃ nupanayhanti, veraṃ tesūpasammati. ^8

Na hi verena verāni, sammantīdha kudācanaṃ; ⤵ Averena ca sammanti, esa dhammo sanantano. ^9

Pare ca na vijānanti, mayamettha yamāmase; ⤵ Ye ca tattha vijānanti, tato sammanti medhagā. ^10

Subhānupassiṃ viharantaṃ, indriyesu asaṃvutaṃ; ⤵ Bhojanamhi cāmattaññuṃ, kusītaṃ hīnavīriyaṃ; ⤵ Taṃ ve pasahati māro, vāto rukkhaṃva dubbalaṃ. ^11

Asubhānupassiṃ viharantaṃ, indriyesu susaṃvutaṃ; ⤵ Bhojanamhi ca mattaññuṃ, saddhaṃ āraddhavīriyaṃ; ⤵ Taṃ ve nappasahati māro, vāto selaṃva pabbataṃ. ^12

Anikkasāvo kāsāvaṃ, yo vatthaṃ paridahissati; ⤵ Apeto damasaccena, na so kāsāvamarahati. ^13

Yo ca vantakasāvassa, sīlesu susamāhito; ⤵ Upeto damasaccena, sa ve kāsāvamarahati. ^14

Asāre sāramatino, sāre cāsāradassino; ⤵ Te sāraṃ nādhigacchanti, micchāsaṅkappagocarā. ^15

Sārañca sārato ñatvā, asārañca asārato; ⤵ Te sāraṃ adhigacchanti, sammāsaṅkappagocarā. ^16

Yathā agāraṃ ducchannaṃ, vuṭṭhī samativijjhati; ⤵ Evaṃ abhāvitaṃ cittaṃ, rāgo samativijjhati. ^17

Yathā agāraṃ suchannaṃ, vuṭṭhī na samativijjhati; ⤵ Evaṃ subhāvitaṃ cittaṃ, rāgo na samativijjhati. ^18

Idha socati pecca socati, pāpakārī ubhayattha socati; ⤵ So socati so vihaññati, disvā kammakiliṭṭhamattano. ^19

Idha modati pecca modati, katapuñño ubhayattha modati; ⤵ So modati so pamodati, disvā kammavisuddhimattano. ^20

Idha tappati pecca tappati, pāpakārī [pāpakāri (?)] ubhayattha tappati; ⤵ "Pāpaṃ me kata"nti tappati, bhiyyo [bhīyo (sī.)] tappati duggatiṃ gato. ^21

Idha nandati pecca nandati, katapuñño ubhayattha nandati; ⤵ "Puññaṃ me kata"nti nandati, bhiyyo nandati suggatiṃ gato. ^22

Bahumpi ce saṃhita [sahitaṃ (sī. syā. kaṃ. pī.)] bhāsamāno, na takkaro hoti naro pamatto; ⤵ Gopova gāvo gaṇayaṃ paresaṃ, na bhāgavā sāmaññassa hoti. ^23

Appampi ce saṃhita bhāsamāno, dhammassa hoti [hotī (sī. pī.)] anudhammacārī; ⤵ Rāgañca dosañca pahāya mohaṃ, sammappajāno suvimuttacitto; ⤵ Anupādiyāno idha vā huraṃ vā, sa bhāgavā sāmaññassa hoti. ^24

Yamakavaggo paṭhamo niṭṭhito. ^25

<h3>2. Appamādavaggo</h3> ^26

Appamādo amatapadaṃ [amataṃ padaṃ (ka.)], pamādo maccuno padaṃ; ⤵ Appamattā na mīyanti, ye pamattā yathā matā. ^27

Evaṃ [etaṃ (sī. syā. kaṃ. pī.)] visesato ñatvā, appamādamhi paṇḍitā; ⤵ Appamāde pamodanti, ariyānaṃ gocare ratā. ^28

Te jhāyino sātatikā, niccaṃ daḷhaparakkamā; ⤵ Phusanti dhīrā nibbānaṃ, yogakkhemaṃ anuttaraṃ. ^29

Uṭṭhānavato satīmato [satimato (sī. syā. ka.)], sucikammassa nisammakārino; ⤵ Saññatassa dhammajīvino, appamattassa [apamattassa (?)] yasobhivaḍḍhati. ^30

Uṭṭhānenappamādena , saṃyamena damena ca; ⤵ Dīpaṃ kayirātha medhāvī, yaṃ ogho nābhikīrati. ^31

Pamādamanuyuñjanti, bālā dummedhino janā; ⤵ Appamādañca medhāvī, dhanaṃ seṭṭhaṃva rakkhati. ^32

Mā pamādamanuyuñjetha, mā kāmaratisanthavaṃ [sandhavaṃ (ka)]; ⤵ Appamatto hi jhāyanto, pappoti vipulaṃ sukhaṃ. ^33

Pamādaṃ appamādena, yadā nudati paṇḍito; ⤵ Paññāpāsādamāruyha, asoko sokiniṃ pajaṃ; ⤵ Pabbataṭṭhova bhūmaṭṭhe [bhummaṭṭhe (sī. syā.)], dhīro bāle avekkhati. ^34

Appamatto pamattesu, suttesu bahujāgaro; ⤵ Abalassaṃva sīghasso, hitvā yāti sumedhaso. ^35

Appamādena maghavā, devānaṃ seṭṭhataṃ gato; ⤵ Appamādaṃ pasaṃsanti, pamādo garahito sadā. ^36

Appamādarato bhikkhu, pamāde bhayadassi vā; ⤵ Saṃyojanaṃ aṇuṃ thūlaṃ, ḍahaṃ aggīva gacchati. ^37

Appamādarato bhikkhu, pamāde bhayadassi vā; ⤵ Abhabbo parihānāya, nibbānasseva santike. ^38

Appamādavaggo dutiyo niṭṭhito. ^39

<h3>3. Cittavaggo</h3> ^40

Phandanaṃ capalaṃ cittaṃ, dūrakkhaṃ [durakkhaṃ (sabbattha)] dunnivārayaṃ; ⤵ Ujuṃ karoti medhāvī, usukārova tejanaṃ. ^41

Vārijova thale khitto, okamokataubbhato; ⤵ Pariphandatidaṃ cittaṃ, māradheyyaṃ pahātave. ^42

Dunniggahassa lahuno, yatthakāmanipātino; ⤵ Cittassa damatho sādhu, cittaṃ dantaṃ sukhāvahaṃ. ^43

Sududdasaṃ sunipuṇaṃ, yatthakāmanipātinaṃ; ⤵ Cittaṃ rakkhetha medhāvī, cittaṃ guttaṃ sukhāvahaṃ. ^44

Dūraṅgamaṃ ekacaraṃ [ekacāraṃ (ka.)], asarīraṃ guhāsayaṃ; ⤵ Ye cittaṃ saṃyamessanti, mokkhanti mārabandhanā. ^45

Anavaṭṭhitacittassa, saddhammaṃ avijānato; ⤵ Pariplavapasādassa, paññā na paripūrati. ^46

Anavassutacittassa, ananvāhatacetaso; ⤵ Puññapāpapahīnassa, natthi jāgarato bhayaṃ. ^47

Kumbhūpamaṃ kāyamimaṃ viditvā, nagarūpamaṃ cittamidaṃ ṭhapetvā; ⤵ Yodhetha māraṃ paññāvudhena, jitañca rakkhe anivesano siyā. ^48

Aciraṃ vatayaṃ kāyo, pathaviṃ adhisessati; ⤵ Chuddho apetaviññāṇo, niratthaṃva kaliṅgaraṃ. ^49

Diso disaṃ yaṃ taṃ kayirā, verī vā pana verinaṃ; ⤵ Micchāpaṇihitaṃ cittaṃ, pāpiyo [pāpiyaṃ (?)] naṃ tato kare. ^50

Na taṃ mātā pitā kayirā, aññe vāpi ca ñātakā; ⤵ Sammāpaṇihitaṃ cittaṃ, seyyaso naṃ tato kare. ^51

Cittavaggo tatiyo niṭṭhito. ^52

<h3>4. Pupphavaggo</h3> ^53

Ko imaṃ [komaṃ (ka.)] pathaviṃ vicessati [vijessati (sī. syā. pī.)], yamalokañca imaṃ sadevakaṃ; ⤵ Ko dhammapadaṃ sudesitaṃ, kusalo pupphamiva pacessati [pupphamivappacessati (ka.)]. ^54

Sekho pathaviṃ vicessati, yamalokañca imaṃ sadevakaṃ; ⤵ Sekho dhammapadaṃ sudesitaṃ, kusalo pupphamiva pacessati. ^55

Pheṇūpamaṃ kāyamimaṃ viditvā, marīcidhammaṃ abhisambudhāno; ⤵ Chetvāna mārassa papupphakāni [sapupphakāni (ṭīkā)], adassanaṃ maccurājassa gacche. ^56

Pupphāni heva pacinantaṃ, byāsattamanasaṃ [byāsattamānasaṃ (ka.)] naraṃ; ⤵ Suttaṃ gāmaṃ mahoghova, maccu ādāya gacchati. ^57

Pupphāni heva pacinantaṃ, byāsattamanasaṃ naraṃ; ⤵ Atittaññeva kāmesu, antako kurute vasaṃ. ^58

Yathāpi bhamaro pupphaṃ, vaṇṇagandhamaheṭhayaṃ [vaṇṇagandhamapoṭhayaṃ (ka.)]; ⤵ Paleti rasamādāya, evaṃ gāme munī care. ^59

Na paresaṃ vilomāni, na paresaṃ katākataṃ; ⤵ Attanova avekkheyya, katāni akatāni ca. ^60

Yathāpi ruciraṃ pupphaṃ, vaṇṇavantaṃ agandhakaṃ; ⤵ Evaṃ subhāsitā vācā, aphalā hoti akubbato. ^61

Yathāpi ruciraṃ pupphaṃ, vaṇṇavantaṃ sugandhakaṃ [sagandhakaṃ (sī. syā. kaṃ. pī.)]; ⤵ Evaṃ subhāsitā vācā, saphalā hoti kubbato [sakubbato (sī. pī.), pakubbato (sī. aṭṭha.), sukubbato (syā. kaṃ.)]. ^62

Yathāpi puppharāsimhā, kayirā mālāguṇe bahū; ⤵ Evaṃ jātena maccena, kattabbaṃ kusalaṃ bahuṃ. ^63

Na pupphagandho paṭivātameti, na candanaṃ tagaramallikā [tagaramallikā (sī. syā. kaṃ. pī.)]; ⤵ Satañca gandho paṭivātameti, sabbā disā sappuriso pavāyati. ^64

Candanaṃ tagaraṃ vāpi, uppalaṃ atha vassikī; ⤵ Etesaṃ gandhajātānaṃ, sīlagandho anuttaro. ^65

Appamatto ayaṃ gandho, yvāyaṃ tagaracandanaṃ [yāyaṃ tagaracandanī (sī. syā. kaṃ. pī.)]; ⤵ Yo ca sīlavataṃ gandho, vāti devesu uttamo. ^66

Tesaṃ sampannasīlānaṃ, appamādavihārinaṃ; ⤵ Sammadaññā vimuttānaṃ, māro maggaṃ na vindati. ^67

Yathā saṅkāraṭhānasmiṃ [saṅkāradhānasmiṃ (sī. syā. kaṃ. pī.)], ujjhitasmiṃ mahāpathe; ⤵ Padumaṃ tattha jāyetha, sucigandhaṃ manoramaṃ. ^68

Evaṃ saṅkārabhūtesu, andhabhūte [andhībhūte (ka.)] puthujjane; ⤵ Atirocati paññāya, sammāsambuddhasāvako. ^69

Pupphavaggo catuttho niṭṭhito. ^70

<h3>5. Bālavaggo</h3> ^71

Dīghā jāgarato ratti, dīghaṃ santassa yojanaṃ; ⤵ Dīgho bālānaṃ saṃsāro, saddhammaṃ avijānataṃ. ^72

Carañce nādhigaccheyya, seyyaṃ sadisamattano; ⤵ Ekacariyaṃ [ekacariyaṃ (ka.)] daḷhaṃ kayirā, natthi bāle sahāyatā. ^73

Puttā matthi dhanammatthi [puttamatthi dhanamatthi (ka.)], iti bālo vihaññati; ⤵ Attā hi [attāpi (?)] attano natthi, kuto puttā kuto dhanaṃ. ^74

Yo bālo maññati bālyaṃ, paṇḍito vāpi tena so; ⤵ Bālo ca paṇḍitamānī, sa ve "bālo"ti vuccati. ^75

Yāvajīvampi ce bālo, paṇḍitaṃ payirupāsati; ⤵ Na so dhammaṃ vijānāti, dabbī sūparasaṃ yathā. ^76

Muhuttamapi ce viññū, paṇḍitaṃ payirupāsati; ⤵ Khippaṃ dhammaṃ vijānāti, jivhā sūparasaṃ yathā. ^77

Caranti bālā dummedhā, amitteneva attanā; ⤵ Karontā pāpakaṃ kammaṃ, yaṃ hoti kaṭukapphalaṃ. ^78

Na taṃ kammaṃ kataṃ sādhu, yaṃ katvā anutappati; ⤵ Yassa assumukho rodaṃ, vipākaṃ paṭisevati. ^79

Tañca kammaṃ kataṃ sādhu, yaṃ katvā nānutappati; ⤵ Yassa patīto sumano, vipākaṃ paṭisevati. ^80

Madhuvā [madhuṃ vā (dī. ni. ṭīkā 1)] maññati bālo, yāva pāpaṃ na paccati; ⤵ Yadā ca paccati pāpaṃ, bālo [atha bālo (sī. syā.) atha (?)] dukkhaṃ nigacchati. ^81

Māse māse kusaggena, bālo bhuñjeyya bhojanaṃ; ⤵ Na so saṅkhātadhammānaṃ [saṅkhatadhammānaṃ (sī. pī. ka.)], kalaṃ agghati soḷasiṃ. ^82

Na hi pāpaṃ kataṃ kammaṃ, sajju khīraṃva muccati; ⤵ Ḍahantaṃ bālamanveti, bhasmacchannova [bhasmāchannova (sī. pī. ka.)] pāvako. ^83

Yāvadeva anatthāya, ñattaṃ [ñātaṃ (?)] bālassa jāyati; ⤵ Hanti bālassa sukkaṃsaṃ, muddhamassa vipātayaṃ. ^84

Asantaṃ bhāvanamiccheyya [asantaṃ bhāvamiccheyya (syā.), asantabhāvanamiccheyya (ka.)], purekkhārañca bhikkhusu; ⤵ Āvāsesu ca issariyaṃ, pūjā parakulesu ca. ^85

Mameva kata maññantu, gihīpabbajitā ubho; ⤵ Mamevātivasā assu, kiccākiccesu kismici; ⤵ Iti bālassa saṅkappo, icchā māno ca vaḍḍhati. ^86

Aññā hi lābhūpanisā, aññā nibbānagāminī; ⤵ Evametaṃ abhiññāya, bhikkhu buddhassa sāvako; ⤵ Sakkāraṃ nābhinandeyya, vivekamanubrūhaye. ^87

Bālavaggo pañcamo niṭṭhito. ^88

<h3>6. Paṇḍitavaggo</h3> ^89

Nidhīnaṃva pavattāraṃ, yaṃ passe vajjadassinaṃ; ⤵ Niggayhavādiṃ medhāviṃ, tādisaṃ paṇḍitaṃ bhaje; ⤵ Tādisaṃ bhajamānassa, seyyo hoti na pāpiyo. ^90

Ovadeyyānusāseyya, asabbhā ca nivāraye; ⤵ Satañhi so piyo hoti, asataṃ hoti appiyo. ^91

Na bhaje pāpake mitte, na bhaje purisādhame; ⤵ Bhajetha mitte kalyāṇe, bhajetha purisuttame. ^92

Dhammapīti sukhaṃ seti, vippasannena cetasā; ⤵ Ariyappavedite dhamme, sadā ramati paṇḍito. ^93

Udakañhi nayanti nettikā, usukārā namayanti [damayanti (ka.)] tejanaṃ; ⤵ Dāruṃ namayanti tacchakā, attānaṃ damayanti paṇḍitā. ^94

Selo yathā ekaghano [ekagghano (ka.)], vātena na samīrati; ⤵ Evaṃ nindāpasaṃsāsu, na samiñjanti paṇḍitā. ^95

Yathāpi rahado gambhīro, vippasanno anāvilo; ⤵ Evaṃ dhammāni sutvāna, vippasīdanti paṇḍitā. ^96

Sabbattha ve sappurisā cajanti, na kāmakāmā lapayanti santo; ⤵ Sukhena phuṭṭhā atha vā dukhena, na uccāvacaṃ [noccāvacaṃ (sī. aṭṭha.)] paṇḍitā dassayanti. ^97

Na attahetu na parassa hetu, na puttamicche na dhanaṃ na raṭṭhaṃ; ⤵ Na iccheyya [nayicche (pī.), nicche (?)] adhammena samiddhimattano, sa sīlavā paññavā dhammiko siyā. ^98

Appakā te manussesu, ye janā pāragāmino; ⤵ Athāyaṃ itarā pajā, tīramevānudhāvati. ^99

Ye ca kho sammadakkhāte, dhamme dhammānuvattino; ⤵ Te janā pāramessanti, maccudheyyaṃ suduttaraṃ. ^100

Kaṇhaṃ dhammaṃ vippahāya, sukkaṃ bhāvetha paṇḍito; ⤵ Okā anokamāgamma, viveke yattha dūramaṃ. ^101

Tatrābhiratimiccheyya, hitvā kāme akiñcano; ⤵ Pariyodapeyya [pariyodāpeyya (?)] attānaṃ, cittaklesehi paṇḍito. ^102

Yesaṃ sambodhiyaṅgesu, sammā cittaṃ subhāvitaṃ; ⤵ Ādānapaṭinissagge, anupādāya ye ratā; ⤵ Khīṇāsavā jutimanto, te loke parinibbutā. ^103

Paṇḍitavaggo chaṭṭho niṭṭhito. ^104

<h3>7. Arahantavaggo</h3> ^105

Gataddhino visokassa, vippamuttassa sabbadhi; ⤵ Sabbaganthappahīnassa, pariḷāho na vijjati. ^106

Uyyuñjanti satīmanto, na nikete ramanti te; ⤵ Haṃsāva pallalaṃ hitvā, okamokaṃ jahanti te. ^107

Yesaṃ sannicayo natthi, ye pariññātabhojanā; ⤵ Suññato animitto ca, vimokkho yesaṃ gocaro; ⤵ Ākāse va sakuntānaṃ [sakuṇānaṃ (ka.)], gati tesaṃ durannayā. ^108

Yassāsavā parikkhīṇā, āhāre ca anissito; ⤵ Suññato animitto ca, vimokkho yassa gocaro; ⤵ Ākāse va sakuntānaṃ, padaṃ tassa durannayaṃ. ^109

Yassindriyāni samathaṅgatāni [samathaṃ gatāni (sī. pī.)], assā yathā sārathinā sudantā; ⤵ Pahīnamānassa anāsavassa, devāpi tassa pihayanti tādino. ^110

Pathavisamo no virujjhati, indakhilupamo [indakhīlūpamo (sī. syā. ka.)] tādi subbato; ⤵ Rahadova apetakaddamo, saṃsārā na bhavanti tādino. ^111

Santaṃ tassa manaṃ hoti, santā vācā ca kamma ca; ⤵ Sammadaññā vimuttassa, upasantassa tādino. ^112

Assaddho akataññū ca, sandhicchedo ca yo naro; ⤵ Hatāvakāso vantāso, sa ve uttamaporiso. ^113

Gāme vā yadi vāraññe, ninne vā yadi vā thale; ⤵ Yattha arahanto viharanti, taṃ bhūmirāmaṇeyyakaṃ. ^114

Ramaṇīyāni araññāni, yattha na ramatī jano; ⤵ Vītarāgā ramissanti, na te kāmagavesino. ^115

Arahantavaggo sattamo niṭṭhito. ^116

<h3>8. Sahassavaggo</h3> ^117

Sahassamapi ce vācā, anatthapadasaṃhitā; ⤵ Ekaṃ atthapadaṃ seyyo, yaṃ sutvā upasammati. ^118

Sahassamapi ce gāthā, anatthapadasaṃhitā; ⤵ Ekaṃ gāthāpadaṃ seyyo, yaṃ sutvā upasammati. ^119

Yo ca gāthā sataṃ bhāse, anatthapadasaṃhitā [anatthapadasañhitaṃ (ka.) visesanaṃ hetaṃ gāthātipadassa]; ⤵ Ekaṃ dhammapadaṃ seyyo, yaṃ sutvā upasammati. ^120

Yo sahassaṃ sahassena, saṅgāme mānuse jine; ⤵ Ekañca jeyyamattānaṃ [attānaṃ (sī. pī.)], sa ve saṅgāmajuttamo. ^121

Attā have jitaṃ seyyo, yā cāyaṃ itarā pajā; ⤵ Attadantassa posassa, niccaṃ saññatacārino. ^122

Neva devo na gandhabbo, na māro saha brahmunā; ⤵ Jitaṃ apajitaṃ kayirā, tathārūpassa jantuno. ^123

Māse māse sahassena, yo yajetha sataṃ samaṃ; ⤵ Ekañca bhāvitattānaṃ, muhuttamapi pūjaye; ⤵ Sāyeva pūjanā seyyo, yañce vassasataṃ hutaṃ. ^124

Yo ca vassasataṃ jantu, aggiṃ paricare vane; ⤵ Ekañca bhāvitattānaṃ, muhuttamapi pūjaye; ⤵ Sāyeva pūjanā seyyo, yañce vassasataṃ hutaṃ. ^125

Porāṇametaṃ atula, netaṃ ajjatanāmiva; ⤵ Nindanti tuṇhimāsīnaṃ, nindanti bahubhāṇinaṃ; ⤵ Mitabhāṇimpi nindanti, natthi loke anindito. ^263

Yaṃ ce viññū pasaṃsanti, anuvicca suve suve; ⤵ Acchiddavuttiṃ [acchinnavuttiṃ (ka.)] medhāviṃ, paññāsīlasamāhitaṃ. ^265

Nikkhaṃ [nekkhaṃ (sī. syā. pī.)] jambonadasseva, ko taṃ ninditumarahati; ⤵ Devāpi naṃ pasaṃsanti, brahmunāpi pasaṃsito. ^266

Kāyappakopaṃ rakkheyya, kāyena saṃvuto siyā; ⤵ Kāyaduccaritaṃ hitvā, kāyena sucaritaṃ care. ^267

Vacīpakopaṃ rakkheyya, vācāya saṃvuto siyā; ⤵ Vacīduccaritaṃ hitvā, vācāya sucaritaṃ care. ^268

Hirīmatā ca dujjīvaṃ, niccaṃ sucigavesinā; ⤵ Alīnenāppagabbhena, suddhājīvena passatā. ^283

Evaṃ bho purisa jānāhi, pāpadhammā asaññatā; ⤵ Mā taṃ lobho adhammo ca, ciraṃ dukkhāya randhayuṃ. ^286

Yassa cetaṃ samucchinnaṃ, mūlaghaccaṃ [mūlaghacchaṃ (ka.)] samūhataṃ; ⤵ Sa ve divā vā rattiṃ vā, samādhimadhigacchati. ^288

Natthi rāgasamo aggi, natthi dosasamo gaho; ⤵ Natthi mohasamaṃ jālaṃ, natthi taṇhāsamā nadī. ^289

Sudassaṃ vajjamaññesaṃ, attano pana duddasaṃ; ⤵ Paresaṃ hi so vajjāni, opunāti [ophunāti (ka.)] yathā bhusaṃ; ⤵ Attano pana chādeti, kaliṃva kitavā saṭho. ^290

Paravajjānupassissa , niccaṃ ujjhānasaññino; ⤵ Āsavā tassa vaḍḍhanti, ārā so āsavakkhayā. ^291

Ākāseva padaṃ natthi, samaṇo natthi bāhire; ⤵ Papañcābhiratā pajā, nippapañcā tathāgatā. ^292

<h3>19. Dhammaṭṭhavaggo</h3> ^295

Na tena hoti dhammaṭṭho, yenatthaṃ sāhasā [sahasā (sī. syā. ka.)] naye; ⤵ Yo ca atthaṃ anatthañca, ubho niccheyya paṇḍito. ^296

Asāhasena dhammena, samena nayatī pare; ⤵ Dhammassa gutto medhāvī, "dhammaṭṭho"ti pavuccati. ^297

Na tena paṇḍito hoti, yāvatā bahu bhāsati; ⤵ Khemī averī abhayo, "paṇḍito"ti pavuccati. ^298

Na tāvatā dhammadharo, yāvatā bahu bhāsati; ⤵ Yo ca appampi sutvāna, dhammaṃ kāyena passati; ⤵ Sa ve dhammadharo hoti, yo dhammaṃ nappamajjati. ^299

Na tena thero so hoti [thero hoti (sī. syā.)], yenassa palitaṃ siro; ⤵ Paripakko vayo tassa, "moghajiṇṇo"ti vuccati. ^300

Yamhi saccañca dhammo ca, ahiṃsā saṃyamo damo; ⤵ Sa ve vantamalo dhīro, "thero" iti [so theroti (syā. ka.)] pavuccati. ^301

Na vākkaraṇamattena, vaṇṇapokkharatāya vā; ⤵ Sādhurūpo naro hoti, issukī maccharī saṭho. ^302

Yassa cetaṃ samucchinnaṃ, mūlaghaccaṃ samūhataṃ; ⤵ Sa vantadoso medhāvī, "sādhurūpo"ti vuccati. ^303

Na muṇḍakena samaṇo, abbato alikaṃ bhaṇaṃ; ⤵ Icchālobhasamāpanno, samaṇo kiṃ bhavissati. ^304

Yo ca sameti pāpāni, aṇuṃ thūlāni sabbaso; ⤵ Samitattā hi pāpānaṃ, "samaṇo"ti pavuccati. ^305

Na tena bhikkhu so hoti, yāvatā bhikkhate pare; ⤵ Vissaṃ dhammaṃ samādāya, bhikkhu hoti na tāvatā. ^306

Yodha puññañca pāpañca, bāhetvā brahmacariyavā [brahmacariyaṃ (ka.)]; ⤵ Saṅkhāya loke carati, sa ve "bhikkhū"ti vuccati. ^307

Pāpāni parivajjeti, sa munī tena so muni; ⤵ Yo munāti ubho loke, "muni" tena pavuccati. ^309

Na tena ariyo hoti, yena pāṇāni hiṃsati; ⤵ Ahiṃsā sabbapāṇānaṃ, "ariyo"ti pavuccati. ^310

Na sīlabbatamattena, bāhusaccena vā pana; ⤵ Atha vā samādhilābhena, vivittasayanena vā. ^311

Phusāmi nekkhammasukhaṃ, aputhujjanasevitaṃ; ⤵ Bhikkhu vissāsamāpādi, appatto āsavakkhayaṃ. ^312

Dhammaṭṭhavaggo ekūnavīsatimo niṭṭhito. ^313

<h3>20. Maggavaggo</h3> ^314

Maggānaṭṭhaṅgiko seṭṭho, saccānaṃ caturo padā; ⤵ Virāgo seṭṭho dhammānaṃ, dvipadānañca cakkhumā. ^315

Tumhehi kiccamātappaṃ, akkhātāro tathāgatā; ⤵ Paṭipannā pamokkhanti, jhāyino mārabandhanā. ^318

"Sabbe saṅkhārā aniccā"ti, yadā paññāya passati; ⤵ Atha nibbindati dukkhe, esa maggo visuddhiyā. ^319

Vanaṃ chindatha mā rukkhaṃ, vanato jāyate bhayaṃ; ⤵ Chetvā vanañca vanathañca, nibbanā hotha bhikkhavo. ^325

Yāva hi vanatho na chijjati, aṇumattopi narassa nārisu; ⤵ Paṭibaddhamanova [paṭibandhamanova (ka.)] tāva so, vaccho khīrapakova [khīrapānova (pī.)] mātari. ^326

Ucchinda sinehamattano kumudaṃ sāradikaṃva [pāṇinā]; ⤵ Santimaggameva brūhaya, nibbānaṃ sugatena desitaṃ. ^327

Idha vassaṃ vasissāmi, idha hemantagimhisu; ⤵ Iti bālo vicinteti, antarāyaṃ na bujjhati. ^328

Taṃ puttapasusammattaṃ, byāsattamanasaṃ naraṃ; ⤵ Suttaṃ gāmaṃ mahoghova, maccu ādāya gacchati. ^329

Na santi puttā tāṇāya, na pitā nāpi bandhavā; ⤵ Antakenādhipannassa, natthi ñātīsu tāṇatā. ^330

Etamatthavasaṃ ñatvā, paṇḍito sīlasaṃvuto; ⤵ Nibbānagamanaṃ maggaṃ, khippameva visodhaye. ^331

Maggavaggo vīsatimo niṭṭhito. ^332

Suppabuddhaṃ pabujjhanti, sadā gotamasāvakā; ⤵ Yesaṃ divā ca ratto ca, ahiṃsāya rato mano. ^344

<h3>22. Nirayavaggo</h3> ^351

Kāsāvakaṇṭhā bahavo, pāpadhammā asaññatā; ⤵ Pāpā pāpehi kammehi, nirayaṃ te upapajjare. ^353

Seyyo ayoguḷo bhutto, tatto aggisikhūpamo; ⤵ Yañce bhuñjeyya dussīlo, raṭṭhapiṇḍamasaññato. ^354

Cattāri ṭhānāni naro pamatto, āpajjati paradārūpasevī; ⤵ Apuññalābhaṃ na nikāmaseyyaṃ, nindaṃ tatīyaṃ nirayaṃ catutthaṃ. ^355

Apuññalābho ca gatī ca pāpikā, bhītassa bhītāya ratī ca thokikā; ⤵ Rājā ca daṇḍaṃ garukaṃ paṇeti, tasmā naro paradāraṃ na seve. ^356

Kuso yathā duggahito, hatthamevānukantati; ⤵ Sāmaññaṃ dupparāmaṭṭhaṃ, nirayāyupakaḍḍhati. ^357

Yaṃ kiñci sithilaṃ kammaṃ, saṃkiliṭṭhañca yaṃ vataṃ; ⤵ Saṅkassaraṃ brahmacariyaṃ, na taṃ hoti mahapphalaṃ. ^358

Kayirā ce kayirāthenaṃ [kayirā naṃ (ka.)], daḷhamenaṃ parakkame; ⤵ Sithilo hi paribbājo, bhiyyo ākirate rajaṃ. ^359

Akataṃ dukkaṭaṃ seyyo, pacchā tappati dukkaṭaṃ; ⤵ Katañca sukataṃ seyyo, yaṃ katvā nānutappati. ^360

Nagaraṃ yathā paccantaṃ, guttaṃ santarabāhiraṃ; ⤵ Evaṃ gopetha attānaṃ, khaṇo vo [khaṇo ve (sī. pī. ka.)] mā upaccagā; ⤵ Khaṇātītā hi socanti, nirayamhi samappitā. ^361

Alajjitāye lajjanti, lajjitāye na lajjare; ⤵ Micchādiṭṭhisamādānā, sattā gacchanti duggatiṃ. ^362

Abhaye bhayadassino, bhaye cābhayadassino; ⤵ Micchādiṭṭhisamādānā, sattā gacchanti duggatiṃ. ^363

Avajje vajjamatino, vajje cāvajjadassino; ⤵ Micchādiṭṭhisamādānā, sattā gacchanti duggatiṃ. ^364

Vajjañca vajjato ñatvā, avajjañca avajjato; ⤵ Sammādiṭṭhisamādānā, sattā gacchanti suggatiṃ. ^365

<h3>23. Nāgavaggo</h3> ^367

Ahaṃ nāgova saṅgāme, cāpato patitaṃ saraṃ; ⤵ Ativākyaṃ titikkhissaṃ, dussīlo hi bahujjano. ^368

Dantaṃ nayanti samitiṃ, dantaṃ rājābhirūhati; ⤵ Danto seṭṭho manussesu, yotivākyaṃ titikkhati. ^369

Varamassatarā dantā, ājānīyā ca [ājānīyāva (syā.)] sindhavā; ⤵ Kuñjarā ca [kuñjarāva (syā.)] mahānāgā, attadanto tato varaṃ. ^370

Na hi etehi yānehi, gaccheyya agataṃ disaṃ; ⤵ Yathāttanā sudantena, danto dantena gacchati. ^371

Dhanapālo [dhanapālako (sī. syā. kaṃ. pī.)] nāma kuñjaro, kaṭukabhedano [kaṭukappabhedano (sī. syā. pī.)] dunnivārayo; ⤵ Baddho kabaḷaṃ na bhuñjati, sumarati [susarati (ka.)] nāgavanassa kuñjaro. ^372

Middhī yadā hoti mahagghaso ca, niddāyitā samparivattasāyī; ⤵ Mahāvarāhova nivāpapuṭṭho, punappunaṃ gabbhamupeti mando. ^373

Idaṃ pure cittamacāri cārikaṃ, yenicchakaṃ yatthakāmaṃ yathāsukhaṃ; ⤵ Tadajjahaṃ niggahessāmi yoniso, hatthippabhinnaṃ viya aṅkusaggaho. ^374

Appamādaratā hotha, sacittamanurakkhatha; ⤵ Duggā uddharathattānaṃ, paṅke sannova [sattova (sī. pī.)] kuñjaro. ^375

Sace labhetha nipakaṃ sahāyaṃ, saddhiṃ caraṃ sādhuvihāridhīraṃ; ⤵ Abhibhuyya sabbāni parissayāni, careyya tenattamano satīmā. ^376

No ce labhetha nipakaṃ sahāyaṃ, saddhiṃ caraṃ sādhuvihāridhīraṃ; ⤵ Rājāva raṭṭhaṃ vijitaṃ pahāya, eko care mātaṅgaraññeva nāgo. ^377

Ekassa caritaṃ seyyo, natthi bāle sahāyatā; ⤵ Eko care na ca pāpāni kayirā, appossukko mātaṅgaraññeva nāgo. ^378

Atthamhi jātamhi sukhā sahāyā, tuṭṭhī sukhā yā itarītarena; ⤵ Puññaṃ sukhaṃ jīvitasaṅkhayamhi, sabbassa dukkhassa sukhaṃ pahānaṃ. ^379

Sukhā matteyyatā loke, atho petteyyatā sukhā; ⤵ Sukhā sāmaññatā loke, atho brahmaññatā sukhā. ^380

Sukhaṃ yāva jarā sīlaṃ, sukhā saddhā patiṭṭhitā; ⤵ Sukho paññāya paṭilābho, pāpānaṃ akaraṇaṃ sukhaṃ. ^381

Nāgavaggo tevīsatimo niṭṭhito. ^382

<h3>24. Taṇhāvaggo</h3> ^383

Manujassa pamattacārino, taṇhā vaḍḍhati māluvā viya; ⤵ So plavatī [plavati (sī. pī.), palavetī (ka.), uplavati (?)] hurā huraṃ, phalamicchaṃva vanasmi vānaro. ^384

Yaṃ esā sahate jammī, taṇhā loke visattikā; ⤵ Sokā tassa pavaḍḍhanti, abhivaṭṭhaṃva [abhivaḍḍhaṃva (syā.), abhivaṭṭaṃva (pī.), abhivuḍḍhaṃva (ka.)] bīraṇaṃ. ^385

Yo cetaṃ sahate jammiṃ, taṇhaṃ loke duraccayaṃ; ⤵ Sokā tamhā papatanti, udabinduva pokkharā. ^386

Taṃ vo vadāmi bhaddaṃ vo, yāvantettha samāgatā; ⤵ Taṇhāya mūlaṃ khaṇatha, usīratthova bīraṇaṃ; ⤵ Mā vo naḷaṃva sotova, māro bhañji punappunaṃ. ^387

Yathāpi mūle anupaddave daḷhe, chinnopi rukkho punareva rūhati; ⤵ Evampi taṇhānusaye anūhate, nibbattatī dukkhamidaṃ punappunaṃ. ^388

Yassa chattiṃsati sotā, manāpasavanā bhusā; ⤵ Māhā [vāhā (sī. syā. pī.)] vahanti duddiṭṭhiṃ, saṅkappā rāganissitā. ^389

Savanti sabbadhi sotā, latā uppajja [ubbhijja (sī. syā. kaṃ. pī.)] tiṭṭhati; ⤵ Tañca disvā lataṃ jātaṃ, mūlaṃ paññāya chindatha. ^390

Saritāni sinehitāni ca, somanassāni bhavanti jantuno; ⤵ Te sātasitā sukhesino, te ve jātijarūpagā narā. ^391

Tasiṇāya purakkhatā pajā, parisappanti sasova bandhito [bādhito (bahūsu)]; ⤵ Saṃyojanasaṅgasattakā, dukkhamupenti punappunaṃ cirāya. ^392

Tasiṇāya purakkhatā pajā, parisappanti sasova bandhito; ⤵ Tasmā tasiṇaṃ vinodaye, ākaṅkhanta [bhikkhū ākaṅkhī (sī.), bhikkhu ākaṅkhaṃ (syā.)] virāgamattano. ^393

Yo nibbanatho vanādhimutto, vanamutto vanameva dhāvati; ⤵ Taṃ puggalametha passatha, mutto bandhanameva dhāvati. ^394

Na taṃ daḷhaṃ bandhanamāhu dhīrā, yadāyasaṃ dārujapabbajañca [dārūjaṃ babbajañca (sī. pī.)]; ⤵ Sārattarattā maṇikuṇḍalesu, puttesu dāresu ca yā apekkhā. ^395

Etaṃ daḷhaṃ bandhanamāhu dhīrā, ohārinaṃ sithilaṃ duppamuñcaṃ; ⤵ Etampi chetvāna paribbajanti, anapekkhino kāmasukhaṃ pahāya. ^396

Ye rāgarattānupatanti sotaṃ, sayaṃkataṃ makkaṭakova jālaṃ; ⤵ Etampi chetvāna vajanti dhīrā, anapekkhino sabbadukkhaṃ pahāya. ^397

Muñca pure muñca pacchato, majjhe muñca bhavassa pāragū; ⤵ Sabbattha vimuttamānaso, na punaṃ jātijaraṃ upehisi. ^398

Vitakkamathitassa jantuno, tibbarāgassa subhānupassino; ⤵ Bhiyyo taṇhā pavaḍḍhati, esa kho daḷhaṃ [esa gāḷhaṃ (ka.)] karoti bandhanaṃ. ^399

Vitakkūpasame ca [vitakkūpasameva (ka.)] yo rato, asubhaṃ bhāvayate sadā sato; ⤵ Esa [eso (?)] kho byanti kāhiti, esa [eso (?)] checchati mārabandhanaṃ. ^400

Niṭṭhaṅgato asantāsī, vītataṇho anaṅgaṇo; ⤵ Acchindi bhavasallāni, antimoyaṃ samussayo. ^401

Vītataṇho anādāno, niruttipadakovido; ⤵ Akkharānaṃ sannipātaṃ, jaññā pubbāparāni ca; ⤵ Sa ve "antimasārīro, mahāpañño mahāpuriso"ti vuccati. ^402

Sabbābhibhū sabbavidūhamasmi, sabbesu dhammesu anūpalitto; ⤵ Sabbañjaho taṇhakkhaye vimutto, sayaṃ abhiññāya kamuddiseyyaṃ. ^403

Sabbadānaṃ dhammadānaṃ jināti, sabbarasaṃ dhammaraso jināti; ⤵ Sabbaratiṃ dhammarati jināti, taṇhakkhayo sabbadukkhaṃ jināti. ^404

Hananti bhogā dummedhaṃ, no ca pāragavesino; ⤵ Bhogataṇhāya dummedho, hanti aññeva attanaṃ. ^405

Tiṇadosāni khettāni, rāgadosā ayaṃ pajā; ⤵ Tasmā hi vītarāgesu, dinnaṃ hoti mahapphalaṃ. ^406

Tiṇadosāni khettāni, dosadosā ayaṃ pajā; ⤵ Tasmā hi vītadosesu, dinnaṃ hoti mahapphalaṃ. ^407

Tiṇadosāni khettāni, mohadosā ayaṃ pajā; ⤵ Tasmā hi vītamohesu, dinnaṃ hoti mahapphalaṃ. ^408

(Tiṇadosāni khettāni, icchādosā ayaṃ pajā; ⤵ Tasmā hi vigaticchesu, dinnaṃ hoti mahapphalaṃ.) [( ) videsapotthakesu natthi, aṭṭhakathāyampi na dissati] ^409

Tiṇadosāni khettāni, taṇhādosā ayaṃ pajā; ⤵ Tasmā hi vītataṇhesu, dinnaṃ hoti mahapphalaṃ. ^410

Taṇhāvaggo catuvīsatimo niṭṭhito. ^411

<h3>25. Bhikkhuvaggo</h3> ^412

Cakkhunā saṃvaro sādhu, sādhu sotena saṃvaro; ⤵ Ghānena saṃvaro sādhu, sādhu jivhāya saṃvaro. ^413

Kāyena saṃvaro sādhu, sādhu vācāya saṃvaro; ⤵ Manasā saṃvaro sādhu, sādhu sabbattha saṃvaro; ⤵ Sabbattha saṃvuto bhikkhu, sabbadukkhā pamuccati. ^414

Hatthasaṃyato pādasaṃyato, vācāsaṃyato saṃyatuttamo; ⤵ Ajjhattarato samāhito, eko santusito tamāhu bhikkhuṃ. ^415

Yo mukhasaṃyato bhikkhu, mantabhāṇī anuddhato; ⤵ Atthaṃ dhammañca dīpeti, madhuraṃ tassa bhāsitaṃ. ^416

Dhammārāmo dhammarato, dhammaṃ anuvicintayaṃ; ⤵ Dhammaṃ anussaraṃ bhikkhu, saddhammā na parihāyati. ^417

Salābhaṃ nātimaññeyya, nāññesaṃ pihayaṃ care; ⤵ Aññesaṃ pihayaṃ bhikkhu, samādhiṃ nādhigacchati. ^418

Appalābhopi ce bhikkhu, salābhaṃ nātimaññati; ⤵ Taṃ ve devā pasaṃsanti, suddhājīviṃ atanditaṃ. ^419

Sabbaso nāmarūpasmiṃ, yassa natthi mamāyitaṃ; ⤵ Asatā ca na socati, sa ve "bhikkhū"ti vuccati. ^420

Mettāvihārī yo bhikkhu, pasanno buddhasāsane; ⤵ Adhigacche padaṃ santaṃ, saṅkhārūpasamaṃ sukhaṃ. ^421

Siñca bhikkhu imaṃ nāvaṃ, sittā te lahumessati; ⤵ Chetvā rāgañca dosañca, tato nibbānamehisi. ^422

Pañca chinde pañca jahe, pañca cuttari bhāvaye; ⤵ Pañca saṅgātigo bhikkhu, "oghatiṇṇo"ti vuccati. ^423

Jhāya bhikkhu [jhāya tuvaṃ bhikkhu (?)] mā pamādo [mā ca pamādo (sī. syā. pī.)], mā te kāmaguṇe ramessu [bhamassu (sī. pī.), bhavassu (syā.), ramassu (ka.)] cittaṃ; ⤵ Mā lohaguḷaṃ gilī pamatto, mā kandi "dukkhamida"nti ḍayhamāno. ^424

Natthi jhānaṃ apaññassa, paññā natthi ajhāyato [ajjhāyino (ka.)]; ⤵ Yamhi jhānañca paññā ca, sa ve nibbānasantike. ^425

Suññāgāraṃ paviṭṭhassa, santacittassa bhikkhuno; ⤵ Amānusī rati hoti, sammā dhammaṃ vipassato. ^426

Yato yato sammasati, khandhānaṃ udayabbayaṃ; ⤵ Labhatī [labhati (pī.), labhate (ka.)] pītipāmojjaṃ, amataṃ taṃ vijānataṃ. ^427

Tatrāyamādi bhavati, idha paññassa bhikkhuno; ⤵ Indriyagutti santuṭṭhi, pātimokkhe ca saṃvaro. ^428

Mitte bhajassu kalyāṇe, suddhājīve atandite; ⤵ Paṭisanthāravutyassa [paṭisandhāravutyassa (ka.)], ācārakusalo siyā; ⤵ Tato pāmojjabahulo, dukkhassantaṃ karissati. ^429

Vassikā viya pupphāni, maddavāni [majjavāni (ka. ṭīkā) paccavāni (ka. aṭṭha.)] pamuñcati; ⤵ Evaṃ rāgañca dosañca, vippamuñcetha bhikkhavo. ^430

Santakāyo santavāco, santavā susamāhito [santamano susamāhito (syā. pī.), santamano samāhito (ka.)]; ⤵ Vantalokāmiso bhikkhu, "upasanto"ti vuccati. ^431

Attanā codayattānaṃ, paṭimaṃsetha attanā [paṭimāse attamattanā (sī. pī.), paṭimaṃse tamattanā (syā.)]; ⤵ So attagutto satimā, sukhaṃ bhikkhu vihāhisi. ^432

Attā hi attano nātho, (ko hi nātho paro siyā) [( ) videsapotthakesu natthi] ⤵ Attā hi attano gati; ⤵ Tasmā saṃyamamattānaṃ [saṃyamaya’ttānaṃ (sī. pī.)], assaṃ bhadraṃva vāṇijo. ^433

Pāmojjabahulo bhikkhu, pasanno buddhasāsane; ⤵ Adhigacche padaṃ santaṃ, saṅkhārūpasamaṃ sukhaṃ. ^434

Yo have daharo bhikkhu, yuñjati buddhasāsane; ⤵ Somaṃ [so imaṃ (sī. syā. kaṃ. pī.)] lokaṃ pabhāseti, abbhā muttova candimā. ^435

Bhikkhuvaggo pañcavīsatimo niṭṭhito. ^436

<h3>26. Brāhmaṇavaggo</h3> ^437

Chinda sotaṃ parakkamma, kāme panuda brāhmaṇa; ⤵ Saṅkhārānaṃ khayaṃ ñatvā, akataññūsi brāhmaṇa. ^438

Yadā dvayesu dhammesu, pāragū hoti brāhmaṇo; ⤵ Athassa sabbe saṃyogā, atthaṃ gacchanti jānato. ^439

Yassa pāraṃ apāraṃ vā, pārāpāraṃ na vijjati; ⤵ Vītaddaraṃ visaṃyuttaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^440

Jhāyiṃ virajamāsīnaṃ, katakiccamanāsavaṃ; ⤵ Uttamatthamanuppattaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^441

Divā tapati ādicco, rattimābhāti candimā; ⤵ Sannaddho khattiyo tapati, jhāyī tapati brāhmaṇo; ⤵ Atha sabbamahorattiṃ [sabbamahorattaṃ (?)], buddho tapati tejasā. ^442

Bāhitapāpoti brāhmaṇo, samacariyā samaṇoti vuccati; ⤵ Pabbājayamattano malaṃ, tasmā "pabbajito"ti vuccati. ^443

Na brāhmaṇassa pahareyya, nāssa muñcetha brāhmaṇo; ⤵ Dhī [dhi (syā. byākaraṇesu)] brāhmaṇassa hantāraṃ, tato dhī yassa [yo + assa = yassa] muñcati. ^444

Na brāhmaṇassetadakiñci seyyo, yadā nisedho manaso piyehi; ⤵ Yato yato hiṃsamano nivattati, tato tato sammatimeva dukkhaṃ. ^445

Yassa kāyena vācāya, manasā natthi dukkaṭaṃ; ⤵ Saṃvutaṃ tīhi ṭhānehi, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^446

Yamhā dhammaṃ vijāneyya, sammāsambuddhadesitaṃ; ⤵ Sakkaccaṃ taṃ namasseyya, aggihuttaṃva brāhmaṇo. ^447

Na jaṭāhi na gottena, na jaccā hoti brāhmaṇo; ⤵ Yamhi saccañca dhammo ca, so sucī so ca brāhmaṇo. ^448

Kiṃ te jaṭāhi dummedha, kiṃ te ajinasāṭiyā; ⤵ Abbhantaraṃ te gahanaṃ, bāhiraṃ parimajjasi. ^449

Paṃsukūladharaṃ jantuṃ, kisaṃ dhamanisanthataṃ; ⤵ Ekaṃ vanasmiṃ jhāyantaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^450

Na cāhaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ brūmi, yonijaṃ mattisambhavaṃ; ⤵ Bhovādi nāma so hoti, sace hoti sakiñcano; ⤵ Akiñcanaṃ anādānaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^451

Sabbasaṃyojanaṃ chetvā, yo ve na paritassati; ⤵ Saṅgātigaṃ visaṃyuttaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^452

Chetvā naddhiṃ [nandhiṃ (ka. sī.), nandiṃ (pī.)] varattañca, sandānaṃ [sandāmaṃ (sī.)] sahanukkamaṃ; ⤵ Ukkhittapalighaṃ buddhaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^453

Akkosaṃ vadhabandhañca, aduṭṭho yo titikkhati; ⤵ Khantībalaṃ balānīkaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^454

Akkodhanaṃ vatavantaṃ, sīlavantaṃ anussadaṃ; ⤵ Dantaṃ antimasārīraṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^455

Vāri pokkharapatteva, āraggeriva sāsapo; ⤵ Yo na limpati [lippati (sī. pī.)] kāmesu, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^456

Yo dukkhassa pajānāti, idheva khayamattano; ⤵ Pannabhāraṃ visaṃyuttaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^457

Gambhīrapaññaṃ medhāviṃ, maggāmaggassa kovidaṃ; ⤵ Uttamatthamanuppattaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^458

Asaṃsaṭṭhaṃ gahaṭṭhehi, anāgārehi cūbhayaṃ; ⤵ Anokasārimappicchaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^459

Nidhāya daṇḍaṃ bhūtesu, tasesu thāvaresu ca; ⤵ Yo na hanti na ghāteti, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^460

Aviruddhaṃ viruddhesu, attadaṇḍesu nibbutaṃ; ⤵ Sādānesu anādānaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^461

Yassa rāgo ca doso ca, māno makkho ca pātito; ⤵ Sāsaporiva āraggā [āragge (ka.)], tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^462

Akakkasaṃ viññāpaniṃ, giraṃ saccamudīraye; ⤵ Yāya nābhisaje kañci [kiñci (ka.)], tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^463

Yodha dīghaṃ va rassaṃ vā, aṇuṃ thūlaṃ subhāsubhaṃ; ⤵ Loke adinnaṃ nādiyati [nādeti (ma. ni. 2.459)], tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^464

Āsā yassa na vijjanti, asmiṃ loke paramhi ca; ⤵ Nirāsāsaṃ [nirāsayaṃ (sī. syā. pī.), nirāsakaṃ (?)] visaṃyuttaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^465

Yassālayā na vijjanti, aññāya akathaṃkathī; ⤵ Amatogadhamanuppattaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^466

Yodha puññañca pāpañca, ubho saṅgamupaccagā; ⤵ Asokaṃ virajaṃ suddhaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^467

Candaṃva vimalaṃ suddhaṃ, vippasannamanāvilaṃ; ⤵ Nandībhavaparikkhīṇaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^468

Yomaṃ [yo imaṃ (sī. syā. kaṃ. pī.)] palipathaṃ duggaṃ, saṃsāraṃ mohamaccagā; ⤵ Tiṇṇo pāragato [pāragato (sī. syā. kaṃ. pī.)] jhāyī, anejo akathaṃkathī; ⤵ Anupādāya nibbuto, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^469

Yodha kāme pahantvāna [pahatvāna (sī. pī.)], anāgāro paribbaje; ⤵ Kāmabhavaparikkhīṇaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ [idaṃ gāthādvayaṃ videsapotthakesu sakideva dassitaṃ]. ^470

Yodha taṇhaṃ pahantvāna, anāgāro paribbaje; ⤵ Taṇhābhavaparikkhīṇaṃ , tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^471

Hitvā mānusakaṃ yogaṃ, dibbaṃ yogaṃ upaccagā; ⤵ Sabbayogavisaṃyuttaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^472

Hitvā ratiñca aratiñca, sītibhūtaṃ nirūpadhiṃ; ⤵ Sabbalokābhibhuṃ vīraṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^473

Cutiṃ yo vedi sattānaṃ, upapattiñca sabbaso; ⤵ Asattaṃ sugataṃ buddhaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^474

Yassa gatiṃ na jānanti, devā gandhabbamānusā; ⤵ Khīṇāsavaṃ arahantaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^475

Yassa pure ca pacchā ca, majjhe ca natthi kiñcanaṃ; ⤵ Akiñcanaṃ anādānaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^476

Usabhaṃ pavaraṃ vīraṃ, mahesiṃ vijitāvinaṃ; ⤵ Anejaṃ nhātakaṃ [nahātakaṃ (sī. syā. kaṃ pī.)] buddhaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^477

Pubbenivāsaṃ yo vedi, saggāpāyañca passati, ⤵ Atho jātikkhayaṃ patto, abhiññāvosito muni; ⤵ Sabbavositavosānaṃ, tamahaṃ brūmi brāhmaṇaṃ. ^478

Dhammapade vaggānamuddānaṃ – ^483

Yamakappamādo cittaṃ, pupphaṃ bālena paṇḍito; ⤵ Arahanto sahassañca, pāpaṃ daṇḍena te dasa. ^484
