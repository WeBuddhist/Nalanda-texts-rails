# Saṅkappo

Iminā puññakammena,

Mā me bāla-samāgamo;

Sataṃ samāgamo hotu,

Yāva nibbānapattiyā.

Idaṃ me puñña kammaṃ

Āsavakkhayaṃ vahaṃ hotu

Sabbadukkhā pamuccatu

Nibbānassa paccayo hotu!

Sādhu! Sādhu! Sādhu!
